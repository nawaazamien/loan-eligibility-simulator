1Tally — loan eligibility simulator · design notes
Structure. One page: compact header with a Demo tag, short title and intro, then a 7/5 grid — form left, estimate right (sticky on desktop) so results sit beside the inputs that produced them. Mobile stacks form → estimate; calculating moves focus and scroll to the estimate heading.
Behaviour. Nothing calculates while typing. Validation runs on submit, then per-field on blur. Editing after a result marks it out of date instead of hiding it. Every artboard below is the live component — click, type and tab through any of them.
Fictional model. 19.5% a year fixed, R69 monthly fee in every repayment, standard amortising loan. Demo limit: the new repayment may use up to 40% of what's left after expenses and existing debt. Full spec in HANDOFF.md.
